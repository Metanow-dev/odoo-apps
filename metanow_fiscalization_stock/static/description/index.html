Warehouse transfer notes for Albania, through efis

An add-on to "AL - Fiskalizimi me efis"

Goods that move inside Albania without a change of owner travel with a warehouse transfer note. This add-on issues it from an Odoo stock transfer: efis numbers the note from the business unit of the warehouse the goods leave from, computes its security code and registers it with the tax authority.

  • Each warehouse names its business unit and its kind of place, chosen from the codes efis confirmed.
  • Each operation type names the transaction its transfers issue. None is preselected.
  • The transfer and the delivery slip show the note's number and both codes.
  • The note is issued today, immediately before the goods move. A resend never issues a second note.

The module does not sign, number or compute a code, holds no certificate or key, and never talks to the tax authority. It needs an efis account, paired through the main module.
