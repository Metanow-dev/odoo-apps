Warehouse transfer notes for Albania, through efis

An add-on to "AL - Fiskalizimi me efis"

Goods that move inside Albania without a change of owner travel with a warehouse transfer note. This add-on issues it from an Odoo stock transfer: efis numbers the note from the business unit of the warehouse the goods leave from, computes its security code and registers it with the tax authority.

  • Each warehouse names its business unit and its kind of place, chosen from the codes efis confirmed.
  • Each operation type names the transaction its transfers issue. None is preselected.
  • The transfer and the delivery slip show the note's number and both codes.
  • The note is issued today, immediately before the goods move. A resend never issues a second note.
  • Goods sent to another person for processing, examination or repair can issue a transport invoice instead, once turned on in Settings.

The module does not sign, number or compute a code, holds no certificate or key, and never talks to the tax authority. It needs an efis account, paired through the main module.

Requires an efis account

efis is an external service run by Metanow. This add-on is its Odoo client and does nothing on its own: your business needs an efis account, and the main module "AL - Fiskalizimi me efis" is what pairs Odoo with it. To get an account, write to support@odoo.metanow.com or visit metanow.com.

What is sent, and when

Nothing leaves Odoo until an administrator pairs the company with efis and an operation type names the transaction its transfers issue. Issuing a note then sends the company's registration codes, the moment it is issued, the value of the goods, the goods themselves, where they leave from and where they go, the vehicle's ownership and plates, and whether the goods are flammable or need an escort. efis registers the note with the Albanian tax authority and returns its number and codes.
