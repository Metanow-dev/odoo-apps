Point of Sale cash for Albania, through efis

An add-on to "AL - Fiskalizimi me efis"

In Albania the cash in a register is declared to the tax authority: the day's opening float, and each time cash is put in or taken out. This add-on declares them from Odoo Point of Sale through efis, which registers them with the authority.

  • Each point of sale names its fiscal device, chosen from the codes efis confirmed.
  • Opening a session declares the day's opening float, once per device and day.
  • Cash in and out during the session is declared as it happens. A declared movement is never deleted.
  • A resend never declares the same movement twice.

The add-on does not fiscalise Point of Sale orders, does not sign or number anything, holds no certificate or key, and never talks to the tax authority.
