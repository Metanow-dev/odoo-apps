Fiscalisation with efis

Albanian fiscalisation of customer invoices, through the efis service

The module sends each posted customer invoice to efis and stores on the invoice what efis returns: the fiscal number, the issuer security code, the registration code and the verification URL. It does not sign, number or compute a security code, it holds no certificate or key, and it never talks to the tax authority. The certificate stays in efis.

Requires an efis account

efis is an external service run by Metanow. This module is its Odoo client and does nothing on its own: you need an efis account for your business, and the module pairs with it using a single-use code issued in the efis web app.

What is sent, and when

Nothing leaves Odoo until an administrator turns fiscalisation on in Settings and pairs the company with efis. From then on, each posted customer invoice is sent to efis: the seller's tax number, address and registration codes, the buyer's name, identifier, address, town and country, and the invoice's lines, taxes, totals and payment method. efis fiscalises it with the Albanian tax authority and returns the result.

Features

  • Checks the invoice as efis would before a number is spent. If efis would not accept it, the posting is rolled back with the list of problems and nothing is sent.
  • Queues accepted invoices and sends them right after posting. Two scheduled actions send the queue and read the states every 5 minutes.
  • Adds a Fiscalisation tab to the invoice with the fiscal state, the fiscal number, the issuer security code, the registration code and the verification URL.
  • Prints the fiscal number, the NIVF, the NSLF and the QR code of the verification URL on the invoice PDF.
  • Filters for invoices waiting for efis and invoices that need action.
  • Never lets an invoice sent for fiscalisation go back to draft, be cancelled or be deleted. It is corrected only by a corrective document.

Limits

  • Odoo 20, Community or Enterprise, on Odoo.sh or self-hosted. Odoo Online cannot load this module.
  • Credit notes and receipts are refused while fiscalisation is on; corrections are issued from efis.
